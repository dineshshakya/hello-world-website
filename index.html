Personalized treatments

Traditional medicine for modern life.

Every plan is built around the individual. Your practitioner may draw from one or several complementary techniques.

01

Acupuncture

Fine, sterile needles are placed at carefully selected points to support your treatment goals.

02

Cupping therapy

Gentle suction is applied to targeted areas to complement acupuncture and hands-on care.

03

Gua sha

A traditional hands-on technique used as part of a thoughtful, individualized session.

04

Moxibustion

A warming technique that may be selected to support a patient’s specific care plan.

05

Electroacupuncture

Gentle electrical stimulation may be paired with acupuncture when appropriate.

06

Herbal medicine

Traditional formulas are considered in the context of your health needs and treatment plan.

07

Auricular therapy

Focused treatment of points on the ear as one part of a broader care approach.

08

Lifestyle guidance

Practical nutrition and wellness recommendations that fit your everyday life.

Your first visit

Know what to expect.

We make room for questions, explain each step, and tailor treatment to what feels right for you.

01 · Listen

A complete conversation

We begin with your current concerns and look at sleep, stress, digestion, and other factors that may be connected.

02 · Plan

Your treatment, explained

Together, we outline an individualized plan and discuss which approaches may be useful for you.

03 · Treat

Calm, unhurried care

Your first appointment includes treatment and is scheduled for about 90 minutes. Follow-ups are about an hour.
